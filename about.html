About the Hub

C2P Tech Hub is new. This page is about what we are building and how it works; not a list of things we have already done.

Why the hub exists

Plenty of theory. Nowhere to build.

Computer engineering students at Ahmadu Bello University finish their degrees knowing a great deal about how systems work in principle. What is harder to come by is somewhere to actually build one: a bench, a deadline, someone more senior to unblock you, and a reason to finish.

C2P Tech Hub exists to close that gap. It is a learning environment in the Department of Computer Engineering where students and curious minds can conceptualize ideas and bring them to life. Our scope spans innovation, incubation, coding and research, and the name states the intent plainly: concept to production.

We were established in the 2025/2026 session, so we are deliberately careful about what we claim. We would rather point at four working projects and one national title than at a long history we do not have.

Our scope

Four things we do

These are not separate departments. Most members touch all four in a single session.

Innovation

Finding the problem worth solving before writing a line of code, and being willing to abandon an idea that does not survive contact with reality.

Incubation

Giving a team that already has something working the structure to take it further: space, a mentor, deadlines, and reviews they have to pass.

Coding

The unglamorous part, done consistently. Weekly sessions, bootcamps, and enough repetition that shipping stops feeling like an event.

Research

Standing clusters working with academic staff on open questions, aimed at citable output rather than at coursework marks.

Common questions

How the hub actually works

Who can join?

Any student of Ahmadu Bello University, at any level from 100L upward. The hub sits inside the Department of Computer Engineering but membership is not restricted to it; we currently have members from Computer Science, Electrical and Electronics Engineering, and Mechatronics Engineering.

Do I need to already know how to code?

No. The introductory bootcamp tracks assume nothing, and the weekly Thursday session has no prerequisite at all. What we do ask for is that you keep turning up; consistency matters far more here than what you already know.

What does it cost?

Membership is free. Development boards and shared machines are provided for bootcamps and are used in pairs. If a project needs components beyond what the hub stocks, that is discussed with the team and the department case by case.

What happens at a Thursday session?

Very little ceremony. People arrive, say what they are stuck on, and get on with it. Some members are working through problem sets, some are pairing on a project, and senior members move between tables. It runs for two hours and there is no register.

How does a project get into an incubation cohort?

You need something that already works, however roughly, and a team that will still be there in four months. Most cohort places go to teams that did well at a hackathon or finished a strong bootcamp project. Eight teams are taken per cohort, and places are lost if milestone reviews are missed.

Where is the hub?

First floor of the Department of Computer Engineering, Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria. Full directions and contact details are on the contact page.

Where we are going

What we are building toward

Stated as intentions, because that is what they currently are. We would rather be held to these than to invented achievements.

A pipeline that runs itself

A member should be able to walk in with no experience and, over two sessions of study, pass through every stage without anyone hand-holding the process.

Research that leaves the building

Clusters producing work that is cited outside the department; conference papers and competition entries, not just final-year project reports.

Tools people outside campus use

ParaVision is the proof this is possible. The target is a steady output of projects that solve a real problem for someone who is not a student.

The easiest way in is to turn up.

The Thursday session needs no registration and no prior experience. If you would rather introduce yourself first, send us your details.
