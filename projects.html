Student Projects

What members have actually built. One national title so far, and four teams currently working toward the next one.

Flagship project

ParaVision

An AI-based diagnostic tool built around a computer vision model trained to identify parasites in microscope imagery. ParaVision detects malaria from blood sample images and intestinal parasites from stool sample images, returning a classification the operator can review rather than a bare yes-or-no answer.

  • Two diagnostic paths from one model family: blood samples for malaria, stool samples for intestinal parasites.
  • Built by students mentored at the hub, not by staff on their behalf.
  • Winner of the innovation track at the Huawei ICT Competition.
