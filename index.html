How it works

Four stages from concept to production

The hub is built around one progression, and the name says it. Every program below feeds the next stage of it.

  1. 1

    Concept

    Arrive with a rough idea, or find one at a Thursday session. Nothing is too small to start with ; most projects here began as a complaint about something that did not work properly.

  2. 2

    Build

    Learn the stack you need in a bootcamp, then build with it. Weekly sessions keep the work moving and put someone who has already solved your problem within arm’s reach.

  3. 3

    Incubate

    Strong builds enter a cohort: bench space, a technical mentor, and fortnightly milestone reviews you have to pass to keep your place.

  4. 4

    Production

    Ship it. That means Demo Day, a competition entry, a conference paper, or a tool that people outside the department actually use.

Flagship project

ParaVision

An AI-based diagnostic tool built around a computer vision model trained to identify parasites in microscope imagery. It detects malaria from blood sample images and intestinal parasites from stool sample images, returning a classification the operator can review rather than a bare yes-or-no answer.

  • Two diagnostic paths from one model family: blood samples for malaria, stool samples for intestinal parasites.
  • Built by students mentored at the hub, not by staff on their behalf.
  • Winner of the innovation track at the Huawei ICT Competition.

What we run

Seven programs, one pipeline

Each program covers a different stage of the arc. Most members start at a Thursday session and work their way along.

Bootcamps

Short, intensive skills sprints that take you from nothing to working code in one stack, closed by defending a small build.

Hackathons

Forty-eight hours, a problem brief published when the clock starts, and a live demo to staff and industry judges at the end.

Incubation Cohorts

A full semester of bench space, a technical mentor and fortnightly milestone reviews you must pass to keep your place.

Flagship program

Weekly Coding Sessions

The hub’s heartbeat. An open drop-in every Thursday with no registration and no prerequisite beyond turning up.

Research Clusters

Standing groups working with academic staff on a defined question in computer vision, embedded systems or networks.

Mentorship Tracks

Junior members paired with senior students and working alumni, matched by focus area and reviewed at mid-semester.

SIWES Placement

Readiness sessions, portfolio help and introductions to partner firms ahead of the industrial placement window.
