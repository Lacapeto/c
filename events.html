22 Sep 2026

Bootcamp Date to be confirmed

Embedded Systems Bootcamp: Cohort 1

A three-week evening bootcamp taking members from bare-metal C to a working sensor node on the ESP32. Sessions are hands-on throughout and each participant defends a small working build in the final week. Boards are provided and shared between pairs.

22 September – 9 October 2026 17:00 – 19:30 WAT, Tuesdays and Thursdays C2P Tech Hub, Ground Floor, Department of Computer Engineering Apply through the Join the Hub form

Apply for this cohort

23 Oct 2026

Hackathon Date to be confirmed

ABU Innovation Hackathon 2026

A 48-hour build sprint against a problem brief published when the clock starts. Teams of three to five have until Sunday afternoon to produce something they can demonstrate live to a panel of departmental staff and visiting industry engineers. The strongest teams are fast-tracked into the next incubation cohort.

23 – 25 October 2026 Opens 16:00 WAT Friday, judging 14:00 WAT Sunday Department of Computer Engineering, Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria Team registration through the Join the Hub form

Register a team

Research Date to be confirmed

Research Seminar: Computer Vision in Diagnostics

The computer vision research cluster presents its current work, using ParaVision as the worked example of how a diagnostic model moves from labelled dataset to something a clinician could use. Open to all members and to staff from across the faculty.

7 November 2026 11:00 – 13:00 WAT Computer Engineering Seminar Room, Ground Floor Open to all members; no registration required

Incubation Cohort 1 Demo Day

C2P Tech Hub Launch

The formal opening of the hub, hosted by the Department of Computer Engineering. Staff, students and invited guests saw the space, the first project benches and the programme of work planned for the session. The first weekly coding sessions began the following week.
