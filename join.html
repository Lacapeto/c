Join the Hub

Membership is open to students of any department and any level, and it costs nothing. Tell us what you want to build and we will find you a bench.

What membership gives you

  • A place to work. Bench space on the first floor, and people around you who are building things too.
  • Seven programs. Bootcamps, hackathons, incubation cohorts, weekly coding sessions, research clusters, mentorship and SIWES placement support.
  • A mentor. Every member is paired with someone further along, reviewed at mid-semester.
  • First call on everything. Bootcamp intakes and hackathon slots open to members before they are announced publicly.

Before you apply

Questions people ask

Do I have to be a Computer Engineering student?

No. The hub sits in the Department of Computer Engineering, but membership is open across departments. Current members come from Computer Engineering, Computer Science, Electrical and Electronics Engineering and Mechatronics Engineering.

Do I need to already know how to code?

No. The weekly coding sessions and the bootcamps exist to take people from nothing to something. What matters is that you turn up consistently; that is the only real entry requirement.

How much time does membership take?

It depends what you sign up for. Weekly coding sessions are one evening a week and are drop-in. Bootcamps run two evenings a week for three weeks. An incubation cohort is a semester-long commitment of roughly six to eight hours a week, and we would rather you joined the next one than half-attended this one.

Is there a fee?

No. Membership and all seven programs are free to students of the university. Hardware for bootcamps is provided by the hub and shared between pairs.

What happens after I submit this form?

Applications are reviewed at the start of each intake. You will be contacted by email with a date for a short conversation about what you want to work on, and then added to the members' channel. If an intake has just closed, your application is held for the next one rather than discarded.

Still deciding?

Come to a Thursday coding session first. They are open, nothing is required of you, and it is the easiest way to see whether the hub is for you.
